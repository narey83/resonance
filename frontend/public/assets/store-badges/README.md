- App Store: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg

These assets retain their owners' trademarks and usage requirements.
Keep badges unmodified, preserve their aspect ratios and clear space, and do not
add store links until the app has an actual listing. The placeholder cards
explicitly state that downloads are not available.
